# The Degenerative

A one-week study made during a Media Lab on the theme *The Degenerative*.

Using a Raspberry Pi, I turned a Squeezebox, a network player designed for music, into a multimedia player. High-frame-rate, high-definition video is cut down to one still image every three seconds, together with its sound. The fragments are queued as one continuous playlist, so the result plays like a low-frame-rate animation.

A device built for audio ends up playing films, and in doing so it turns a *rich image* into a *poor image*. Each frame stays on screen long enough to be looked at, and what the video loses becomes visible. It is a short detour from [Peeker](post.html?id=peeker) that asks the same question from the other side: what happens to an image as it passes through a machine?
