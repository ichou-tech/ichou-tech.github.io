# Some Variations on a Counterfunctional Digital Camera

## In one line
The pictorial looks at the digital camera and the photograph from a **counterfunctional** design perspective, and uses the camera as a way into a broader theme: designing digital limitation.

## What they did
The works are not presented as documentation of a design process, but as visual-textual design artifacts in their own right, combining text, theory and visual design. The format is simple, yet they made a lot.

## What stays with me
Even though it is an older piece, the idea of counterfunctionality is still inspiring and a useful reference: once you stop optimising for function, things become playful.

## Open questions
- Are these all concepts and prototypes rather than fully built devices?
