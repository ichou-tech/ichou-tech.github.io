# Beyond Charts: A Conceptual Framework for Sketch-based Data Visualisations
[ACM DL](https://dl.acm.org/doi/full/10.1145/3765712.3765722)

## In one line
Hand-drawn visualisations (think *Dear Data*, a reference I love) are highly expressive but tedious and error-prone, while digital tools limit our visual vocabulary and creativity. The paper looks for a way to extend the expressiveness of data visualisation.

## How it is built
A critical analysis of existing tools: problem, tools, gaps. Section 4 then proposes a prototype, a compositional grammar for sketch-based visualisations. There is no user study.

## A lead I want to follow
The paper builds on what Mackay and Beaudouin-Lafon call **interaction substrates**: a layer that holds the objects a user creates and manages the constraints and relationships between them (a grid that aligns what you drop in it, a timeline that orders things in time). Behind it is their long-standing principle of **reification**, from *Reification, Polymorphism and Reuse* (2000): turning what is hidden inside a system, here the constraints themselves, into objects users can see and manipulate.

## Open questions
