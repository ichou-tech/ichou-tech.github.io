# LastWar

A card game with one simple gesture: swipe left or right. You play from a god's-eye view, and your goal is to postpone the Third World War for as long as possible.

## Six agents, no script

The world has six factions, and each one is an independent AI agent. Their representatives come to you with events and ask what they should do. Each agent reacts to your earlier choices and generates its questions spontaneously, conditioned on its own history and on the current state of the timeline.

Because the dilemmas are not written in advance, the game is highly open-ended: the space of possible timelines is practically unbounded, and there is no fixed limit to how long war can be delayed.

## Why

LastWar is an experiment in emergent narrative with multi-agent systems: what happens when the story is not authored but negotiated between a player and several agents with their own logic? It also asks the player to sit with decisions that have no clean answer, one swipe at a time.
