# Voice Lesson Planner

A Claude skill I designed for learning languages with AI, built as a multi-session workflow.

## How it works

- **A planning session.** You tell it your goals, background and anything else that should shape the course. It drafts a syllabus and builds an artifact that keeps track of your progress, mistakes and personal vocabulary list.
- **A daily lesson plan.** Each day, based on how the last lesson went, the planner writes the next one.
- **A fresh teacher every day.** You copy the plan in one click into a new voice-mode session, and that session is your teacher. Opening a new voice session for each lesson keeps contexts from piling up.

## Why

Long single conversations drift as context accumulates. Splitting planning and teaching into separate sessions, with a persistent record in between, is a small design pattern for human-AI workflows. In my own testing it clearly improved how personal the lessons felt and their overall quality.
